# Code of Conduct

We want Lemqo's community to be respectful, welcoming, and focused on useful collaboration. Treat people with courtesy, discuss ideas without personal attacks, and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Follow the rules of any space where the project is discussed, and respect others' privacy and time.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Contact a maintainer through the repository's GitHub contact channels to report a concern. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
